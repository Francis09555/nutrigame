How To Play

⌨ Move

Use WASD or Arrow Keys. Your hero attacks automatically.

💨 Dash

Press Space to dash with brief invulnerability.

⬆ Grow

Collect green XP orbs. Pick one of three upgrades each level.

👑 Win

Survive the timer, then defeat the stage boss.

🧠 Learn

Answer nutrition quizzes for healing, XP and score.

⏸ Pause

Press Escape or P. Touch controls appear on mobile.

Tip: evolved weapons require a max-level weapon and its matching passive.
